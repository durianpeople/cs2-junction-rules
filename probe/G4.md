# G4: "check the logs of this junction later too, it's weird" (JR-T-013b)

Owner shot d33a790e-9dde-481e-aaab-2daa63c562c9.

## Which junction

Bluejay St x Alderwood St. This is an unboxed T at about (-1758.8, 271.2) (x, z), 136 m east of the boxed
Alderwood x Alderbrook 4-way from G3. The location comes from the Carto export and the street names in the shot.

## What the shot shows

- Bluejay is queued through the T in both directions.
- The game's stuck icon sits on Bluejay just west of the T.
- A car from Alderwood is nosing into the mouth.
- There is no box here, so nothing stops cars from entering the junction when their exit is full.

## What the logs say

Nothing about this junction. The 0.4.6 log only has city-wide counters every 1024 frames. It names a node only
when a box is saved, and this T has no box. So the log cannot tell what held whom here. A small diagnostic was
added (below).

## Plain explanation for the owner (likely, not yet confirmed)

This looks like queues backing up through the junction, not a Junction Rules hold:

1. Alderwood west of the T leads to the boxed Alderbrook junction (G3).
2. When a right-turner stood across the oncoming lane there (the G3 bug), Alderwood stopped moving. The queue
   reached back the 136 m to this T.
3. Bluejay cars turning into Alderwood could not get in, so they stood in the T.
4. That blocked Bluejay through traffic in both directions, and the side-road car could only nose in.

An unboxed junction lets cars queue across it. The game shows the stuck icon when a car has waited a long time.

The G3 fix should remove the main cause. If it still happens, the watch below will tell.

## New: watch one junction (debug, default off)

Settings, Debug group:

- **Watch the junction at camera centre (log)**: point the camera at the junction, then press it. Any junction
  works, boxed or not. It uses the same pick as the paint test's "Add junction at camera centre".
- **Stop watching the junction.**

While a junction is watched, and while the Junction Rules copy drives, every car standing there writes a line
to the mod log. "Standing there" means its current lane belongs to the junction, or it is checking a lane of the
junction ahead.

Example line:

`watch junction Entity(N:V): car Entity(..) stands (0.0 m/s, limit 0.0 m/s) on lane Entity(..); waits for Entity(..) (Continuing); rule: game's own (give-way / car ahead / signal, see blocker)`

- **waits for** is the vehicle the game says the car waits for, with the game's blocker type. Continuing means
  the car ahead. Crossing means a give-way or a crossing car. Signal means a red light. "no vehicle named" means
  the game names no vehicle, for example a give-way to a lane reservation or a Junction Rules hold.
- **rule** is which rule set the car's limit:
  - yellow box: at the line;
  - yellow box: turn spot, oncoming;
  - yellow box: turn spot, no exit room (G3);
  - entrance band;
  - zebra: people, room or vehicles;
  - whole junction: waits for a later conflict, stopped inside, or safety stop;
  - building exit;
  - otherwise the game's own.

Limits: at most one line per car, rule and blocker every 512 steps, and 20 lines per 256 steps. A summary line
counts the rest. The start and stop lines name the node and its position, and say whether it is boxed.

Other behaviour:

- Watching switches the per-car counter results on, so the log may say `junction gap counters on`.
- There is no driving effect and nothing is saved. Loading a city stops the watch.

## What the owner checks in game

1. Point the camera at Bluejay x Alderwood and press "Watch the junction at camera centre (log)".
2. Check the log for the line `watch junction: watching node Entity(..) at (..) (no box)`.
3. When it looks weird again, read the lines.
   - Mostly `rule: game's own` with `Continuing` on the lanes into Alderwood means spillback from the west
     (the explanation above).
   - `whole junction: ...` rules mean a Junction Rules hold. Send that log.
4. Press "Stop watching the junction" afterwards.
