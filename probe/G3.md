# G3: right-turner in a yellow box holds the main road (JR-T-013b)

Junction: Alderwood St x Alderbrook St, boxed 4-way, node Entity(373495:1) at (-1896.5, 594.3, 272.6)
(box placed 23:01:19 in the 0.4.6 log). Owner shot fdd93a0b-7453-4c9e-af6b-b89150ebcc84.

## What was wrong

A lorry turning right (left-hand traffic: across the oncoming lane) waited at its turn spot inside the box
(the UK right-turn exception). When the oncoming gap came, it crossed even though its exit (Alderbrook, up arm)
had filled while it waited. It stopped behind the exit queue with its cab across the oncoming lane. The
main-road straight traffic then waited for it. That is the game's own collision check against a vehicle that
stands on its path (decompile `Game.Simulation/CarLaneSpeedIterator.cs` 1352-1388).

The priority was never inverted. The timing log showed `priority changed 0` all session, and a turner waiting at
its spot does not hold oncoming cars (the stock skip at the same file, line 1382).

Root cause: the box exit-room test ran only at the stop line. A car already in the box counts as "Inside" and
is never tested again:

- `Stage2/JRYellowBox.cs` 88-91 (`inBox`, then `Classify`)
- `Core/BoxDriving.cs` 53

The turn spot (`Stage2/JRWholeJunction.cs` 390-394) only judged the oncoming points.

The exit filled for these reasons:

- the exit queue grew while the lorry waited;
- oncoming left-turners took the room;
- the boxed car exits on Alderbrook north (about 20 m and 70 m from the node) hold that queue about 16 m out.

## What changed

New block S2-35, `BoxTurnRoomHold`. While the turn spot is the turner's next spot and it can still stop before
it, the room test runs again. If there is no room on its exit, it stays at the turn spot, which is before the
first oncoming overlap.

The test reads only the turner's own path: the rest of its lane from its front, then its exit lanes. It never
reads an oncoming lane. So the wait ends when the exit moves, not when oncoming traffic does. This adds no new
wait cycle.

The rule applies only when all of these hold:

- "Cars obey yellow boxes" is on;
- "Yellow boxes: turning across oncoming traffic may wait in the box" is on;
- the gap rule is Whole junction.

Settings: none new.

## Log

In the timing line ("Log navigation timing" on), a new count appears at the end, after the building-exit part
and before `points at W`:

`; right-turn waits for exit room N` = car-steps in which a turner stood at its turn spot because its exit was full.

`right-turn waits N` (the oncoming wait) is unchanged.

## What the owner checks in game

1. Watch Alderwood x Alderbrook at busy time.
2. A right-turner may wait inside the box. When its exit (Alderbrook up arm) is full, it now stays at its turn
   spot, clear of the oncoming lane, even when there is a gap. Main-road straight traffic keeps flowing past it.
3. It goes when there is a gap and room on its exit.
4. Timing line: `right-turn waits for exit room` is above 0 at busy times, and the main road does not lock.
5. Optional: aim the G4 watch at this node. The lines then say `rule: yellow box: turner waits at its turn spot, no room on its exit`.

## Open points

- A long vehicle's rear may still sit over an earlier crossing (the same as 0.4.2).
- A turner waits as long as its exit stays full. Cars behind it in the same lane wait too (UK practice).
- An oncoming left-turner can still take the exit room first. That is stock priority.
