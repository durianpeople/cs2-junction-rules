# G1: vehicles leaving buildings still cut out (JR-T-013a)

Branch `jr-t-013a`, off 8bf6cf2 (0.4.6-probe). Version not bumped. Managed build only; nothing was installed.

## What was wrong

In 0.4.6 every timing window showed `hand-overs to the road from: lot 0, gate 0, other 2..28`. The 'other' path is a
building's **spawn location object**:

- The game starts the building's path at the spawn location entity itself, so the vehicle's first navigation lane is a
  `TransformTarget` (Game.Pathfind/PathfindTargetSeeker.cs 427-479, ~680; CarNavigationSystem.cs 1078-1091).
- The spawn location is tied to the nearest lane within 32 m, which is the public road lane
  (Game.Objects/SpawnLocationConnectionSystem.cs 400-448, 535-620).
- The stock `TransformTarget | ParkingSpace` branch never looks at the road lane. The vehicle stays hidden
  (VehicleUtils.CheckUnspawned 1806-1821) until it is placed straight onto the road lane, with no gap check and no box
  check.

The same branch carries three more ways out that were never judged:

- cars leaving building parking or garages (`Connection | ParkingSpace`)
- cars pulling out of **kerbside** parking
- cars leaving a building's own road where it meets a public junction

`cap hits` was the scan's work cap, not the 63-box limit:

- 0.4.6 limit: 24 lanes and 96 vehicles per decision.
- Every vehicle on a lane counted against the 96, even ones already past the exit or far away.
- A cap hit counted as "go", so the vehicle pulled out.

## What changed

- **S2-35** (spawn point and parking): the vehicle waits at its spawn point (hidden) or in its parking space. It uses the
  same decision as every other exit:
  - gap judgement on the lane it joins and on every lane it crosses
  - then, at a boxed exit, room past the band
- **S2-36** (building road): a vehicle on a building's own road brakes and stops 1 m before the public junction, using
  the same decision. A signalled arm follows its own signal instead.
- **S2-33**: every vehicle held at an exit stays on its lane. The game cannot hand it over to the road while it waits.
- **Junction joins** (lot, building road or spawn point joining a junction lane): the crossing points now come from the
  joined lane's own overlaps. The old rule compared lanes side by side, which only works on a straight road.
- **Caps**:
  - Only vehicles still ahead of the exit and within reach count against the 96-vehicle cap.
  - Hitting the vehicle cap now means "busy" (wait).
  - The lane cap went from 24 to 48 lanes. Hitting it still means go, because that cap depends on the road layout, not
    on traffic.
- **Kerbside parking is included**, by the owner rule "a building exit is a yielding arm": a car moving off gives way.
- **Not hooked**: a vehicle whose path starts directly on the road lane (the game's fallback for a building with no
  spawn point). It appears in the lane and drives as normal road traffic. A truck stopped in its lane is already in
  traffic.

## Settings

- **Vehicles leaving buildings give way** (existing, default on). It now covers every way out listed above.
- **Exit trace (log)** (new, Debug group, default **off**). Needs the setting above and the Junction Rules copy.
  - Writes one line when a vehicle starts waiting at an exit, and one when it joins the road.
  - At most 20 lines a second. Every second the log says how many lines were dropped or lost.

## Log lines

The timing line (debug timing on) keeps its 0.4.6 text. These parts are added at the end:

```
..., hand-overs to the road from: lot L, gate G, other O, spawn point S, building parking P, kerbside parking K, building road B, drive D; hand-overs not judged U; cap hits by kind: lanes X (go), vehicles Y (wait)
```

- `other` now means only the paths that are still not classified (expected 0). In 0.4.6 it included the spawn points.
- `hand-overs not judged` counts paths the rule should judge that went onto the road without a decision. Expected 0.
  On-duty emergency vehicles are not included.

Trace lines:

```
exit trace (log) on: one line per vehicle leaving a building or parking space (first wait, hand-over to the road), at most 20 lines a second
exit trace: waits vehicle 123456:3 building 789:2 path spawn point (spawn location object) road owner 55 decision wait (gap) boxed yes frame 4096
exit trace: joins road vehicle 123456:3 building 789:2 path spawn point (spawn location object) road owner 55 decision go (gap clear) boxed yes frame 4160
exit trace: 37 lines dropped (rate limit), 0 records lost (ring overrun)
```

Possible decision values:

- `wait (gap)`
- `wait (box: no room past)`
- `wait (dense traffic: vehicle cap)`
- `go (gap clear)`
- `go (gap clear; lane cap hit, far lanes not read)`
- `go (on-duty emergency, stock)`
- `judged before the hand-over (driveway give-way)` (drives)
- `not judged` (should not appear for a judged path)

## What the owner checks in game

The new build has to be Burst-built and installed by main first.

1. Turn on **Log navigation timing**. In each window check:
   - `spawn point` and/or `building parking` / `kerbside parking` are above 0 (this was the 0.4.6 `other`).
   - `other` is 0 or close to it.
   - `hand-overs not judged` is 0.
   - `waits` is clearly higher than 0.4.6's ~5 per window.
2. **Alderwood boxed exits** (the 0.4.6 screenshot), with a queue past the box in the far lane:
   - A truck turning right out of the building must not enter the box.
   - It waits hidden at its spawn point until there is room past the box and a gap.
3. A busy road with kerbside parking: parked cars pull out only into a gap.
4. Turn on **Exit trace (log)** for a minute near Alderwood:
   - Each leaving vehicle shows a `waits` line and/or a `joins road` line.
   - The truck at the boxed exit should show `wait (box: no room past)` or `wait (gap)`.
   - Any `not judged` line names the path and lane type that is still uncovered.
5. Watch for deadlocks: the rule has no time limit. If an exit onto a road that never clears holds vehicles, or a
   kerbside-parked car never gets out, report the trace line.
