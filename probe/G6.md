# G6: no parking in yellow boxes (0.4.7, JR-T-013d)

Owner rule (17 Nov 2026): no car parks inside a yellow box, whether or not the road has kerbside parking there
(shot 3aa10c18: cars parked in an exit box's kerbside parking on Crescent Street, both sides).

## What changed

- New setting **No parking in yellow boxes** (Options, Junction Rules, boxes group). Default **on**.
- Every parking slot of the road under a box is closed: junction boxes and exit boxes, both sides of the road.
  - A slot counts as under a box if its space overlaps the painted box outline by more than 10 cm sideways.
  - The game closes these slots the same way it closes slots at a driveway. Cars looking for a space skip them, and the
    pathfinder no longer counts them as free space.
- Cars already parked in a box are not moved. They leave when their owner next drives off; nothing is teleported or
  despawned.
- Runtime only:
  - Nothing new is saved with the city.
  - Before every save (manual, autosave, save on quit) the blocked lanes get back the game's own free-space value.
  - After the save, the box blocks are back.
- Removing a box, turning the setting off, or painting stopping opens the slots again within a frame.
  Painting runs whenever saved boxes exist.

## Log lines (Player.log / the mod log)

- On every change:
  `yellow boxes: no parking in boxes ON: N box(es), L parking lane(s) with R blocked range(s) (S marked space(s) on M slotted lane(s)); O lane(s) opened again; U lane(s) recomputed; x ms`
- When the setting goes off: the same line with `OFF`.
- Each save:
  - Before: `yellow boxes: no parking in boxes: before the save, L parking lane(s) carry the game's own free space (C changed)`
  - After: `... after the save, L parking lane(s) recomputed with the boxes again`
- On failure: `yellow boxes: no parking in boxes failed while ...; switched off until the game restarts, parking in the boxes opened again`.
  Send that log to the Technical Ministry.

## What to check in game

1. **Crescent Street exit box (shot 3aa10c18).**
   - Cars already parked inside the box drive off over the next in-game hours. No new car parks in the box, on either
     side of the road.
   - Slots just outside the box are still used.
2. **A junction box on a road with kerbside parking.**
   - No car parks in the box part of the kerb.
   - The slots beyond the box edge still fill.
3. **Arriving cars.**
   - Watch a car heading for a space near the box. It parks outside the box, or it drives on and finds another space.
   - It does not stop in the box and does not wait there.
4. **Remove a box with the tool.**
   - Parking there works again: cars park in those slots later.
   - The log shows `L lane(s) opened again`.
5. **Setting off, then on.**
   - Off: cars may park in boxes again.
   - On: the slots are closed again, and parked cars stay until they leave.
6. **Save safety.**
   - Save the city with boxes (manual save, and let an autosave run). Check both log lines appear.
   - Disable the mod, load that save: kerbside parking under the old boxes is intact. Cars park there as in 0.4.6, with
     no error, and no lane shows as full.
7. **Road upgrade under a box.**
   - Upgrade or replace the road (or let Traffic / RoadBuilder rewrite its lanes).
   - The box is rebuilt (paint log), and the parking block comes back (no-parking log line).
   - No parked car is moved because of the mod.
